 GENERAL
 AI – ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
 Allowed usage of AI:
  Development of an AI: If it is given by the task of the thesis e.g.,
   “develop a neural network to…”, the usage of AI is allowed.  The usage
   need to be explained in detailed technical steps in several pages, as this is
   the core of the thesis
  AI is used to improve the text writing like an improved spellchecker OR AI
   is used as a challenger for your technical questions e.g. “Any drift near
   threshold?”  Following things need to be added to the thesis:
      Which AI is used e.g., ChatGPT
      Which model e.g., GPT-4.5
      Copy each used prompt as machine readable text (no screenshot!) to the
       attachment.
      If you have uploaded a file to the prompt, you need to add the file to
       your deliverables of the thesis or refer the public source of the file.
27/10/2025   THD | NuW | road to thesis - a guideline about successfully manage your thesis | Prof. Dr. Matthias Górka   page 12
 GENERAL
 AI – ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
 Not allowed usage of AI (leading to a failed thesis!):
    Using the AI for text improvements without mentioning it
     and/or without adding the details (see page before)
    A completely written content by AI e.g. “Dear AI write
     me the chapter of…” is not allowed. A thesis need to be
     created by yourself, and you need to be the originator.

 GENERAL
 AI – ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
 Be aware:
  Don’t upload personal or confidential data to the AI!
  You are accountable for the usage of the AI output – wrong / bad output is
   your fault, not fault of the AI.
  If you spending low effort for the prompt writing, you will get a low-quality
   output.
  Don’t trust any of the AI output! The AI is also hallucination outputs that
   look correct but are 100% fake. This includes even fake sources of papers.
  You need the knowledge of the output to verify if the output can be used.

 HYPOTHESIS – DEFINITION
  Is the core of your whole thesis!
  A hypothesis is an assumption that is not yet proven  If I can
   easy Google / look into a book and find the answer to the
   assumption, your hypothesis is not valid!
  A hypothesis is a connection between different aspects and
   variables
  A hypothesis goes beyond the current existing state of the art
   knowledge
  The thesis must contribute to academical research, not only
   implementation or development and therefore promote the state-
   of-the-art research knowledge to a next level. So, the hypothesis
   goes beyond the current existing state of the art knowledge.
27/10/2025   THD | NuW | road to thesis - a guideline about successfully manage your thesis | Prof. Dr. Matthias Górka   page 21
 GENERAL
 HYPOTHESIS – NEEDS TO BE…
  Logical connection: IF A rise, fall,… THEN B…
  Directed:
     Not direct: the number of wires has an impact to the reliability of the transformer
     directed: an increase of the number of wires has a negative impact to the reliability
      of the transformer
  Universal: not limited to one specific case
  Objective: no personal opinion
  Measurable: A hypothesis is needs to be verified within the thesis (not in the
   abstract) by real measurement or minimum a simulation to proof the hypothesis [not just
   literature research!]. So, measurements / simulation must be part of the thesis!
  Precise
  Short: maximum of ~20 words

 FILE LABELING
  All files send to the Professor need to have the format:
             [YYMMDD]_[type]_[content]_v[version]
  No space “ “ is allowed  use “_”
  For example:
          221209_schematic_high_voltage_transformer_v001
  NOT:
     221209_schematic high voltage transformer_1

 EXAMPLE
  Requirement: “Current charging technologies have the
   disadvantage of roughly low efficiency (~80%). Goal is, to
   develop a charging device for 500W peak power with an
   efficiency of >95% at its operating point.”
  Tests:
    1. Peak power test at normal environment (25°C, standard
         humidity) for 30min at 12V input voltage, 24V output
         voltage. OK-Criteria: minimum of 500W output power
    2. Efficiency measurement (output to input power) at
         250W output power, 12V input voltage, 24V output
         voltage, after 5min run and at normal environment (25°C,
         standard humidity). OK-Criteria: minimum 95% Efficiency

 WRITING THE THESIS
 PROVE OF THESIS
 (Hypo)thesis are logical consistent and is based on the what,
  why and prove, e.g.
    Wrong:
              I choose Arduino.
      Still wrong:
              I choose Arduino because it’s the easiest to use platform.
      Correct:
              I choose Arduino because it’s the easiest to use platform as proven
               in the Tech Journal[Reference literature]
              I choose Arduino because it’s the easiest to use platform. The
               following chapter shows the investigation and comparison about the
               usability of the different platforms.

  MASTER SEMINAR

  The master's seminar consists of two parts that must be
   passed to successfully complete the module.
  To prepare for the master's thesis, participation in the seminar
   series "Career Start into German Technology Companies" is
   mandatory.
  The seminars / workshops are offered as block events during
   the first two semesters of study.
  The events cover a variety of topics that are of great
   importance for the preparation of the Master's thesis.
  The second part is the master colloquium.

     Add chapter headline on each page so that we know in which

  RATING OF THESIS
                                                                                                                         Why?




 WHAT WILL BE TAKEN INTO ACCOUNT?
  Meeting culture (joined in time, prepare yourself,…)
  Milestones and content delivered in time (no delay) – exceeding the
   set due dates by 1 day or more results in a lower grad!
  Content is according to standards e.g., schematics are in
   professional tools, not power point!
  Complexity of the topic.
  Scientific & methodical approach.
  Set targets achieved.
  Topic is handled self driven and proactive
  Structured working on the topic.
  Paper written properly.

  RATING OF THESIS
                                                                                                                         Why?




 WHAT WILL BE TAKEN INTO ACCOUNT?
  Written thesis itself
     Correct / no missing cite
     Structure of thesis itself
     List of references existing, complete and sorted by appearance
     List of tables existing, complete and sorted by appearance
     List of pictures existing, complete and sorted by appearance
     List of abbreviations existing, complete and sorted alphabetic
     Appropriate literature is searched, and state of the art is fully
      described.
     Hypothesis are logical consistent.
     Results are evaluated critical
