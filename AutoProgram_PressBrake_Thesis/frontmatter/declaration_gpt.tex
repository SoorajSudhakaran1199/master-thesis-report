\chapter*{Declaration on the Use of AI Tools}

During the preparation of this thesis, ChatGPT (model family: GPT-5) was used
exclusively for language refinement, grammar checking, formatting support, and
LaTeX-related assistance. The technical analysis, system design,
implementation, interpretation of results, and all scientific conclusions were
developed independently by the author.

All prompts used for thesis preparation are documented in machine-readable form
in the appendix prompt log and are to be included in the final submission
files in accordance with the applicable thesis guideline.
