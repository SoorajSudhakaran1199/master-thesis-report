\noindent
\textbf{Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI)}

\noindent
During the preparation of this thesis, generative AI tools (ChatGPT, OpenAI, GPT-5 model family) were used in a limited and supportive capacity. This included language refinement, grammar correction, text structuring, formatting support, LaTeX assistance, and consistency checking.

\noindent
AI tools were not used to generate experimental data, perform technical analysis, design the system, implement algorithms, or derive scientific conclusions. All technical content, including system design, implementation, evaluation, and interpretation of results, was developed independently by the author.

\noindent
All AI-assisted content was reviewed and validated by the author, who assumes full responsibility for the submitted work. A record of AI usage is documented in the appendix prompt log.
