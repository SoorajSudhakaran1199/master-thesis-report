\textbf{Functional Requirement (SMART)} &

\hline

FR-00 &
The system shall generate an execution-ready drag\&bot robot program from workpiece geometry, configuration data, and operator bend-side input for the evaluated press-brake scenarios. &
For valid evaluated inputs, the generated workflow shall be ready for simulated execution without manual waypoint teaching or function-block sequencing; unresolved infeasible cases shall stop in a controlled way rather than continue blindly. &
Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation-validation} workflow comparison and scenario validation \\
\hline
FR-01 &
The system shall detect unreachable or infeasible target poses before robot motion commands are dispatched. &
Unreachable or unsafe target poses shall be rejected before unsafe motion execution and shall generate warning or error feedback for operator decision-making. &
Workspace-constrained and failure-prone validation \\

\hline

FR-02 &
The system shall allow the operator to select the required bend side before automated program generation or execution. &
The selected bend side shall be reflected in the generated workflow without manual resequencing, including Side~A direct bending and Side~E opposite-side bending. &
Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation-validation} Side~A/Side~E validation \\

\hline

FR-03 &
The system shall automatically insert a regrip operation when the current workpiece pose cannot directly reach the next target pose. &
The regrip logic shall be inserted when direct execution is infeasible and shall execute or stop safely in regrip-required and opposite-side scenarios without manual waypoint construction. &
Regrip-required and Side~E scenario validation \\

\hline

FR-04 &
The system shall handle changes in initial workpiece orientation through automatic orientation validation and correction. &
The automated workflow shall complete the evaluated orientation-variation scenario without manual correction of the generated sequence. &
Automated orientation-variation trials \\

\hline

FR-05 &
The system shall support opposite-side bending by generating the required flip and reorientation workflow automatically. &
The opposite-side workflow shall generate flip and regrip behaviour automatically and shall complete the evaluated Side~E scenario when no unresolved workspace or fixture constraint remains; otherwise it shall stop in a controlled way. &
Side~E opposite-side workflow trials \\

\hline

FR-06 &
The system shall determine grip positions that satisfy edge-clearance, hole-avoidance, and holding-force checks before execution. &
For evaluated valid workpiece states, selected grips shall pass geometric and holding-force checks before trajectory execution; invalid grips shall be rejected. &
\acs{tc}-06 grip-planning test \\

\hline

FR-07 &
The system shall detect unsafe conditions and either replan or stop execution in a controlled way before unsafe continuation. &
Unsafe cases shall be handled through warning feedback, replanning where possible, or controlled stop before unsafe continuation; a controlled stop is safe system behaviour but an incomplete execution trial. &
Failure-prone scenario validation \\
